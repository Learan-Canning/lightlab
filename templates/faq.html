Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

Ordering

Place your order directly through our website by adding products to your basket and completing checkout. Once payment is confirmed, you will receive an order confirmation by email.
We accept secure online card payments through our checkout provider. Available payment options are shown at checkout before you complete your order.
If your order has not been processed yet, we may be able to amend or cancel it. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and request.

Shipping & Delivery

Dispatch and delivery times depend on your location and selected shipping method. Estimated delivery windows are shown at checkout and in your confirmation email.
International shipping availability depends on destination and local regulations. If your country is eligible, shipping options will appear during checkout.
Free shipping may be offered during promotions or above certain order values. Any active free-shipping threshold is shown at checkout.

Quality & Documentation

Yes. We provide certificates of analysis for applicable products to support transparency and batch-level verification.

Storage & Handling

Store products according to the guidance provided on the product page or label. In general, keep items sealed, dry, and away from heat and direct light.
Shelf life varies by product type and storage conditions. Refer to product-specific guidance and documentation for the most accurate timeframe.
